# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 12 November 2026*

Red Panda is a to-do and notes app for iPhone and iPad by Mas Datos GmbH. Its source code is public at
[github.com/os-mas-datos/red_panda](https://github.com/os-mas-datos/red_panda), so anyone can check what this page says.

## In short

**Red Panda collects no data.** There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs
and no accounts. Red Panda has no server of its own: nothing you write, say or do in the app is sent to us
or to anyone else. Red Panda never records where you go.

## What stays on your device

Your notes, to-dos, voice recordings, settings and the panda's bamboo are stored in the app's storage on
your device. Deleting Red Panda deletes them.

## iCloud sync (optional)

If you switch on iCloud sync, your notes, voice recordings, categories and the bamboo are copied to your own iCloud Drive, in a
folder only you can read. Apple stores them under the [iCloud terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/icloud/).
We have no access to your iCloud. Your location and your settings are never synced.

## Location: only for the leave basket

The leave basket reminds you of the items in it when you leave the place where you filled it. For that:

- Red Panda asks iOS once for your current position and asks iOS to watch a circle around it.
- iOS alone watches for you leaving the circle and wakes Red Panda when you do. Red Panda does not follow
  you and does not record where you go.
- The centre of the circle is kept only on your device, and only until the reminder fires or you empty the
  basket. It is never sent anywhere and never synced.
- "Always" location permission is needed only because iOS can wake a closed app for this circle only with
  it. You can use "While using" instead; the basket then only reminds you while Red Panda is open.
- The "When my Wi-Fi drops" trigger needs no location permission at all.

## Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only while you record a voice note. Your speech is turned into text on the device
wherever iOS supports this for your language. Otherwise iOS uses Apple's speech recognition, under
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The recording itself stays on your device
(and in your iCloud, if sync is on).

## Notifications, Siri, widgets and sharing

Reminders are local notifications created on your device. Siri, Shortcuts, the widgets, the Lock Screen and
Control Centre buttons and the share extension all run on your device and only read or write your notes.

## Children

Red Panda collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. You stay in full
control: delete notes in the app, delete the app to remove everything on the device, and remove the iCloud
copy in iOS Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage > Red Panda.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version is published here and in the public history of the
repository. Red Panda will not start collecting data.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at
[github.com/os-mas-datos/red_panda/issues](https://github.com/os-mas-datos/red_panda/issues).
Issues are public, so please don't include personal details.
