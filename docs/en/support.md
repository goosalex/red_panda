# Support

Red Panda is a to-do and notes app for iPhone and iPad. Type or dictate a note, swipe it away when it's
done, and keep the panda happy.

## Questions and problems

Open an issue at [github.com/os-mas-datos/red_panda/issues](https://github.com/os-mas-datos/red_panda/issues).
Please say which iPhone or iPad, which iOS version and which Red Panda version (Settings > News) you use.
Issues are public, so please don't include personal details.

## Frequently asked

**The leave basket doesn't remind me when I leave.**
In Red Panda, open Settings > Leave basket and check the location permission. With "While using", iOS only
tells Red Panda that you left while the app is open; "Always" lets iOS wake it. For Wi-Fi, add the
Shortcuts automation that Settings > Leave basket > "When I leave a Wi-Fi" shows you.

**Voice notes don't record.**
Red Panda needs the microphone and speech recognition permissions. The blue links in the app take you to
the right place in iOS Settings.

**My notes don't appear on my other devices.**
Switch on Settings > iCloud sync in Red Panda on each device, and check that iCloud Drive is on in iOS
Settings.

**How do I add Red Panda to the Lock Screen, Home Screen, Control Centre or Action Button?**
Settings > Quick capture shows each place, with a walkthrough.
